Civil Engineer - Construction Site Operations
Kochi, Kerala

About the role
We are looking for a civil engineer to run day-to-day construction site
operations for an ongoing residential project. You will be the site
engineer of record, reporting to the project manager.

Requirements
- 3+ years managing construction site operations is required
- Strong Primavera P6 scheduling experience is mandatory
- BOQ preparation and rate analysis required
- Civil 3D and AutoCAD for site layouts required
- HSE and quality management responsibility essential
- Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering required

Nice to have
- Primavera P6 certification is a plus
- Exposure to dbt or SQL for progress reporting is a plus
- Strong communication skills in English and Malayalam
